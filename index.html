What happens inside

  1. SenseThe sensor array reads gases from the food.
  2. ProcessRaw readings become checked, usable values.
  3. DecideFixed, rule-based thresholds set the result.
  4. ConnectThe result is sent to the app over Bluetooth Low Energy.
  5. Tell youDisplay, voice and sound all give the same answer.

Built to fail safe

A dropped connection, a sensor fault or an invalid reading is never shown as safe food. TasteBud shows an error instead. It is a Food Freshness Assessment Tool, not a guarantee of food safety, and temperature, humidity and strong food aromas can affect readings.

Designed for everyone from the start

Accessibility is core to TasteBud, not an add-on. Anyone who cannot rely on sight or smell can still get a confident answer.

Braille and tactile controls

Embossed control surfaces, inspired by medicine packaging, for blind and low-vision users.

Voice and sound

Spoken guidance plus a distinct sound for every result.

Multi-sense feedback

Built for people with anosmia, post-viral smell loss or colour blindness, so no single sense is required.
