# Ivory

## Company Profile
 
Ivory is an innovative medical and consumer technology enterprise dedicated to eliminating preventable food waste and foodborne illnesses. 
By pairing advanced molecular sensing with universal, inclusive product engineering, Ivory delivers reliable, objective freshness insights into everyday homes and professional institutions.

## Vision Statement

"To create a world where food is never wasted and every meal is safe, empowering households and institutions through accessible, intelligent technology that guards human health and preserves the planet's vital resources."

## Mission Statement

"Our mission is to develop and deliver ShelfSense as an intuitive, affordable, pocket-sized device providing rapid, lab-grade food freshness and spoilage assessment. 
By combining cutting-edge volatile compound detection with universal, inclusive design featuring braille, tactile cues, and audio feedback we empower everyday consumers, older adults, and individuals with sensory impairments to make confident food safety decisions, drastically reduce avoidable waste, and lower household grocery costs."
 
## Our flagship innovation
 
Tastebud is a handheld food freshness and spoilage assessment device. 
The device replaces subjective sensory guesswork—such as visual appearance, smell, andarbitrary calendar dates—with calibrated molecular detection.
 
## Projects
 
Universal accessibility- Braille controls, audio feedback sensors, and sensory impairment solutions.
Mobile application- Barcode/QR integration, smart shelf-life tracking, and consumer feedback.
